    Demo of the Nimbus request-and-reply flow. The storm, its track, warnings, evacuation sites, class suspensions, flood-prone areas and road reports are made-up examples, and the maps are schematic sketches, not real hazard maps. The LGU console is a mock: nothing is sent and no sign-in is checked. A real reply depends on cell signal, SMS load and gateway speed.
